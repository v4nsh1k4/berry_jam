## What the game delivers

- **Core loop:** steal words (speech bubbles) from comic characters and speak
  them as abilities: OPEN, PUSH, REMEMBER, HELP, WAIT, HUSH, HIDE, plus a
  final story word. Wrong words never fail hard; words are never used up.
- **Light:** a mouse-aimed flashlight that reveals hidden clues and solves
  light puzzles (ink that recedes, a lens, a shadow puzzle, light-revealed
  levers), while filling a "noticed" meter that draws the Ink Crawler.
- **Enemy:** the Ink Crawler (patrol, stalk, hunt, telegraphed lunges, hiding
  spots, search), the Ink Shadow chase, and a listening guardian.
- **Comic damage:** every stolen word cracks, bleeds and glitches the page and
  leaves characters unfinished; returning words heals it.
- **Twist:** a reveal sequence recasting the monster and the player, then a
  return phase where every word must be given back.
- **Content:** 3 chapters, about 20 comic-panel rooms incl. a four-panel page
  spread; 6 cutscenes, an intro and an ending; dial locks, clues and light
  puzzles; 8 one-time scares.
- **Presentation:** all art drawn in code (ink line boil, halftone, panel
  transitions), synthesized music and sound, title page, menus, About,
  Controls, Credits, pause, save/continue, restart chapter.
- **Platform:** browser (HTML5) build for itch.io.
