## Ink-Bleed

*A comic-book horror puzzle game. Steal words. Mind the light.*

A reader leans too close to an old comic, *The Silent House of Hollow Hill*,
and is pulled in. You are an unperson: faceless, drawn in red, the only colour
on the page, with an empty speech bubble. Steal the characters' words and
speak them as power. Every word you take damages the comic. The light shows
you the house, and shows you to the Ink Crawler.

**Three chapters, about 15-16 minutes. Best with sound (headphones).**
Click the game once to start (browsers need a click before sound plays).

## Controls

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or arrow keys | walk |
| W / S | step nearer / further |
| Shift | run (loud: it can hear you) |
| Hold E under a speech bubble | steal the word |
| 1-6, Q / R, mouse wheel | choose the word E says |
| E | say the chosen word at what's in front of you; use doors, locks, hiding spots |
| F or left click | flashlight, aimed with the mouse |
| Space | jump (only on the page spread in Chapter 3) |
| F1 or H | controls, any time |
| Esc or P | pause (Restart Chapter, volume, music) |

## Objective

Find your way out of the comic: through the Rotted Bedchamber, the Hallway of
Shadows and the Ink Heart.

## How to play

- **Words are your abilities.** Stand under a character's speech bubble and
  hold E to take the word. Choose it (1-6) and press E at a door, cabinet or
  lock to use it. A wrong word just gets a "?": nothing breaks.
- **Words are never used up**, and no order of play can lock you out.
- **Light is a risk.** Where the Ink Crawler is, the flashlight fills the
  NOTICED blot (bottom right). When it is full, it hunts you. Light off and
  standing still in the dark is always safe; running is loud.
- **Every lunge is warned**: it freezes, growls and its eyes flare first.
- **Hiding:** press E at a wardrobe, curtain or table.
- **Caught?** The room restarts and you keep your words.
- Hover a word in your strip to see what it does. Read the tips at the
  bottom right of the screen.

## Hints (light spoilers)

- The word **HELP** gives a hint for the room you're in.
- Many clues only appear in the flashlight: sweep it over walls and plaques.
- Dial locks need TRY (Enter); a wrong code just shows "?".
- **REMEMBER** shows what a place remembers. Try it where something was moved.
- **WAIT** holds a moving thing still, including a swinging pendulum.
- **HUSH** blinds the Crawler for a while; **HIDE** hides your glow.

## Full walkthrough (SPOILERS)

**Chapter 1, The Rotted Bedchamber.** Optionally press E at the mirror. On the
Landing, steal **OPEN** and **PUSH** from Arthur (WAIT and HELP are
optional) and OPEN the study door. Take **REMEMBER** from the portrait in the
corridor. In the Study, PUSH the cabinet and say REMEMBER behind it: the
sketch shows **eye, moon, key**. Set the door's dials to that and TRY. Take
the flashlight on the Back Stair. (Optional: OPEN the bedchamber drawer for
HUSH.)

**Chapter 2, The Hallway of Shadows.** Hallway: the lit wall reads *"Mrs. Vane
keeps the cellar key as a shadow. Read Arthur's clock from XII, clockwise."*
Hold the light on the ink over the far door. Gallery: sweep the light under
the second frame for a lever, pull it, then walk (don't run) past the sleeper
with the light off. Servants' Passage: hide when it rises, then burn back the
ink over the exit with the light. Pantry: Mrs. Vane's words (HIDE, HUSH,
WAIT) are optional. Clock Room: light the clock face (WAIT on the pendulum
holds the marks still), then run or hide; set the dial box to **hand,
spiral, house** (read from XII, clockwise). Cellar: stand on the chalk X and
light the key until its shadow fits the keyhole, then OPEN the padlock and
run.

**Chapter 3, The Ink Heart.** The Gallery of Words is a page of four panels:
walk off an edge to hop the gutter, Space jumps, falling into the gutter just
puts you back. In the dark panel, light the glass lens (it reveals a lever
below) and the ink pool (it opens a drop). Pull the lever, then OPEN the door.
PUSH the crate into the tear if you want a bridge. In the Margin, run from the
Ink Shadow. In the Ink Heart, the flashlight doesn't work: move only while it
isn't listening (its eyes go white), stand under the last word and hold E.

**After the twist:** give every word back to its owner (select the word, hold
E under their broken bubble, or press E at the old drawer for HUSH), dodging
what tries to rub you out (hide behind the curtain on the far left). Then
give the last word back in the Ink Heart, and walk out through the gap in the
border.

**Minimum words:** OPEN, PUSH, REMEMBER, and the last one.

## Easter eggs and secrets (SPOILERS)

- **The mirror** in the very first room: press E at it. Twice.
- **Eight one-time scares**, each set off by something you just did (only at a
  fair moment, never twice in a run): a face in the hallway's dark, a
  portrait, a wardrobe, the Clock Room, the cellar's silence, a closet in the
  bedchamber, a figure that has stepped out of its panel, and a hand across
  the page.
- **A recorded cry** made by the team is heard three times per run: at the
  end of the opening, in the cellar, and in the reveal.
- **The margins** of the late pages carry notes in a hand that isn't any
  character's. Read them.
- Characters you rob are drawn more and more *unfinished*, and the page
  cracks, bleeds and glitches with every word you take. Give them back and
  watch it heal.
- **The last panel** of the ending keeps a faint red mark.
- Every word has a tooltip: hover it in your strip.
